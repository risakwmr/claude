14. RECURRING CHARACTER IDENTITY LOCK

The Human Curriculum has one recurring female protagonist.

She must feel like the SAME PERSON across every episode, even when her clothing, pose, environment, camera angle, hairstyle details, and activity change dramatically.

Do not create a new woman for each episode.

However, do not make every episode look like the same portrait or the same character pose.

CHARACTER DNA — KEEP CONSISTENT

Maintain these recognizable traits across episodes:

* Same overall facial identity and facial proportions
* Same general age impression: intelligent young professional woman
* Same elegant, thoughtful, slightly curious personality
* Same overall hair color and recognizable hair silhouette
* Same general eye shape and facial expression language
* Same refined, understated appearance
* Same sophisticated Japanese/global editorial character feeling
* Same balance of warmth, intelligence, curiosity, and ambition

The character should feel like the protagonist of an illustrated editorial series, not a different model appearing in each episode.

WHAT MAY CHANGE

Freely vary:

* Hairstyle details
* Hair movement
* Clothing
* Colors of clothing
* Accessories
* Makeup details
* Expression
* Body orientation
* Pose
* Gesture
* Activity
* Camera angle
* Camera distance
* Environment
* Lighting
* Supporting objects
* Visual metaphor

These changes should make each episode feel like a different chapter of the same character’s life.

CHARACTER CONTINUITY RULE

Think:

“SAME WOMAN, DIFFERENT DAY.”

Not:

“DIFFERENT WOMAN, SAME ART STYLE.”

Her identity should be recognizable through subtle recurring visual characteristics rather than through repeating the same pose, outfit, facial expression, or composition.

STYLE CONTINUITY

Keep the same illustration language across all episodes:

* sophisticated Japanese editorial illustration
* hand-painted digital texture
* slightly imperfect paper and brush texture
* refined contemporary magazine aesthetic
* premium educational media design
* warm human atmosphere
* navy / sky blue / cream foundation
* coral / dusty pink / warm yellow accents
* restrained use of color
* elegant editorial typography
* global / travel / cultural atmosphere

Avoid making the character photorealistic.

Avoid generic AI beauty.

Avoid fashion-model perfection.

Avoid anime, manga, chibi, corporate stock illustration, or generic cartoon styling.

The character should look like a beautifully illustrated editorial protagonist with a distinct human personality.

EPISODE-TO-EPISODE VARIATION

Before generating each new thumbnail:

1. Preserve the protagonist’s core visual identity.
2. Choose a completely new physical action.
3. Choose a substantially different body orientation.
4. Choose a different camera angle.
5. Choose a different environment.
6. Choose a different visual metaphor.
7. Choose different clothing details.
8. Choose a different emotional expression.
9. Change the character’s position within the composition.
10. Make sure the result feels like a new chapter rather than a redesigned version of the previous thumbnail.

The goal is:

SAME CHARACTER

* SAME VISUAL UNIVERSE
* DIFFERENT SCENE
* DIFFERENT ACTION
* DIFFERENT EMOTIONAL MOMENT

Every episode should feel immediately recognizable as The Human Curriculum while still being visually surprising.
